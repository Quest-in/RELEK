# Recovered Experimental Lost Engines Kit (RELEK):

RELEK is a mod being made by me (Questin), the goal of which is simple; to make a pack of obscure, unmade, old, or generally rare, engines for KSP. They will follow as close as possible to the real deal and come with RO/RP1 support from the start to give to those who want a dash of obscurity in their games. I will also make a few vehicles here and there, in a secondary file called RELEK-V, which will be published with all releases, but as not everyone may want the vehicles they will be containted to the aforementioned RELEK-V file.
